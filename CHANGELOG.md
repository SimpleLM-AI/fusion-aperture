# Changelog

## Fusion Aperture evaluation pack · 19 August 2026

First locked pack for Fusion Aperture.

RULER summaries at 4K, 8K, 16K, 32K, 64K, 128K, and 1,048,576 tokens, n=10 per task, greedy decode, stock NVIDIA `evaluate.py` output. Null answers 0/10 at every length.

NIAH public summary of 110 cells. Three misses, all at 1M.

OpenAI MRCR v1, two needle, 512K to 1M, 91 items.

Physical footprint table for RULER 32K through 1M and for MRCR. NIAH footprint omitted (too few polls). 4K through 16K RULER footprint omitted (mixed earlier watcher).

Paper included as markdown and PDF. Verification scripts check hashes and recompute headline averages.

Product surface is stated as iPhone 12 and later, A14 Bionic and later chips and devices. Quality and physical memory were measured on Mac.
