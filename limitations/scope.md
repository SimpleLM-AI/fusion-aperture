# Scope

This pack measures Fusion Aperture as shipped, on one frozen Qwen3.5-2B Q4_K_M host, under three public harnesses, with physical memory on a Mac.

It does not describe Fusion Aperture. It does not prove a new attention algorithm. It does not transfer the Mac footprint to iPhone 12 and later, or to A14 Bionic and later chips and devices. It does not rank trained 80B class models on RULER at 1M. It does not replace NVIDIA’s n=500 protocol with n=10.

The 500 MB product budget and the measured envelope are not the same number. See `measurements/memory.md`.
