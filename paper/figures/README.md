Figures in this folder are drawn from the CSVs in `results/` and from `measurements/memory.md`. They are illustrations of those tables, not extra measurements.

`ruler_context_curve.png` plots the unweighted 13 task mean and NIAH-8 against context length.

`memory_context_curve.png` plots median physical footprint with min to max bars on the trusted RULER sweeps. The dashed line at 500 MB is the product budget, not a measured ceiling.

`niah_ladder.png` is the 10 by 11 pass/fail grid. Three misses at 1M sit on the 30%, 70%, and 90% rows, which are the grid slots for the recorded depths 33.91%, 66.09%, and 89.57%.

`mrcr_comparison.png` places our 0.326 on the OpenAI two needle neighbourhood. MiniCPM’s 28.62 is omitted because it is a 128K cell.
